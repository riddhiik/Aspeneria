PCOS/PCOD

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) or Polycystic Ovarian Disease (PCOD) is a hormonal disorder affecting women. It is characterized by irregular periods, excess hair growth, and acne.

Symptoms of PCOS/PCOD

  • Irregular periods or missed periods
  • Excess hair growth on the face, chest, and abdomen (hirsutism)
  • Acne
  • Weight gain or obesity
  • Thinning hair or hair loss
  • Darkening of the skin in folds (acanthosis nigricans)
  • Infertility

Causes of PCOS/PCOD

The exact cause of PCOS/PCOD is unknown, but it is believed to be due to a combination of factors, including:

  • Hormonal imbalances
  • Insulin resistance
  • Genetics

Treatment for PCOS/PCOD

Treatment for PCOS/PCOD depends on the specific symptoms and goals of the individual. Common treatment options include:

  • Lifestyle changes
  • Medication
  • Surgery

Additional Information

It's important to consult with a healthcare professional for a proper diagnosis and treatment plan.
